# Play Store checklist

- [ ] Privacy Policy URL: `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/privacy-policy.html`
- [ ] Account deletion URL (Data safety form): `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/privacy-policy.html#delete`
- [ ] In-app account deletion: Account (login) button > "Delete my account"
- [ ] Refund policy: No refund (stated in the app and on the policy page)
- [ ] Health apps declaration: state that the app is not a medical device and gives no diagnosis
- [ ] Data safety form: location (optional), photos (processed on device), app activity, account info; third parties: Firebase, Google sign-in, AI service
- [ ] Content rating and target audience: 18+ (child tests are for parents/guardians)
- [ ] Emergency helplines shown in app: Tele-MANAS 14416, KIRAN 1800-599-0019, 112
