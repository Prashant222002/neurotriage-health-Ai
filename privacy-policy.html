Privacy Policy

NeuroTriage is a pre-clinical mental health screening and self-help app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor.

  • Guest mode: no account is needed and your data stays on your device.
  • With an account: only game scores, task counts, friends and leaderboard names sync to the cloud. Sensitive data such as mood, tests, diary and SOS contacts goes to the cloud only if you choose Doctor-care sharing.
  • Auto-saved progress: your name, age and answers are saved in your device's browser so you can continue later. They are removed when your report is made or when you delete saved progress.
  • Photo scan (OCR): runs on your device. Photos are not uploaded or saved.
  • Amisha chat: the text you type and the last few messages are sent to our server and an AI service to create a reply. Please do not type your full name, phone number or address.
  • Doctor-care (optional): summaries you choose are visible only to the verified professional you consent to, for the period you set. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Nearby clinics: your approximate location is sent to OpenStreetMap (Overpass) only when you use that feature.
  • Third-party services: Google sign-in, Firebase, Google Fonts, cdnjs, YouTube and an AI service. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.
  • Children: the app is mainly for ages 18+. Child-related tests should be filled by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
  • Permissions: location, camera/photo or microphone are requested only when you use the matching feature and can be turned off in phone settings.

Refund Policy (No Refund)

Once a payment is made for any paid service of NeuroTriage, such as the Doctor-care subscription or any other plan, it is non-refundable. No full or partial refund is given, even if you stop using it midway, do not use the plan, or delete your account. Free features are not charged.

To stop renewal, cancel your plan where you bought it (for example Google Play > Subscriptions). Cancelling stops future charges but earlier payments are not refunded. The only exception is where the law, or Google Play's own refund policy for Play purchases, separately requires one.

Delete your Account and Data

  • In the app: open the Account (login) button, tap "Delete my account" and tap again to confirm. Your account and its cloud data are removed permanently. To remove only cloud data, use "Delete my cloud data".
  • On your device: open the Privacy screen and tap "Delete all data on this device".
  • By email: if you cannot delete from the app, write from your registered email to drpsuryawanshidm@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account and data as soon as possible.
  • Reports or files you already downloaded or shared are outside our control.
