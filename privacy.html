Privacy Policy

NeuroTriage – Your Mental Health Companion · Effective 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information the NeuroTriage app and website (neurotriage.in) handle, why, and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

NeuroTriage is not a medical device. It is a self-help, screening and research tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor, psychologist or psychiatrist.

1. What we collect

Guest mode (no account)

You can use much of the app without an account. In Guest mode your entries stay on your own device, in the browser or app storage.

If you create an account

  • Account details: email address, name and sign-in method (Google, Apple or email).
  • Synced activity: game scores, task counts, friends and leaderboard name.

Health-related entries

Screening answers, mood, diary, thought records, medicines, test results and SOS contacts. By default these stay on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to back up or sync them, or if you turn on Doctor-care sharing (section 4).

Chat with Amisha

When you talk to Amisha, the text you type and a few recent messages are sent to our server and to an AI service so that a reply can be created. Please do not type your full name, phone number, address or other identifying details in the chat.

Photo scan (OCR)

Photo scanning runs on your device. Your photo is not uploaded or saved. Some helper files may download from the internet to make the scan work.

Approximate location

Only when you search for nearby clinics, your approximate location is sent to OpenStreetMap (Overpass) to find results. We do not store it.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay (web) or Google Play (Play Store). We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We keep only the order ID, plan, amount and dates.

Technical data

Like any app or website, the services we use can see basic technical data such as IP address, device type and app version. If you allow notifications, a device token may be used to send them.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app and show your results, progress and reports.
  • To sync and back up your data when you sign in.
  • To unlock the plan you bought and prevent fraud.
  • To reply to support requests.
We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not use your health entries for advertising.

If you share or download a report, it is under your control only until you share it. After that we cannot control the file. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Doctor-care sharing (optional)

If you choose this feature, only the summaries you select (for example medicines, mood, tests, tools) become visible to the verified professional you consented to, for the period you set. You can withdraw consent at any time from the app.

5. Storage, security and deletion

  • Guest data stays on your device until you clear it. Tap “Delete saved progress” or “Forget my saved details and consent” in the app, or clear your browser or app data.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Delete everything: open Account in the app and tap Delete account. Your account, synced data and plan record are erased. Payment records that tax and accounting law require us to keep are retained without your health data.
  • If you cannot use the app, email us from your registered address and ask for deletion. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • A Play Store subscription is not cancelled by deleting the app or account. Cancel it first in Play Store, Payments & subscriptions.

No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login private.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or export your data, withdraw consent, delete your data, or make a complaint. Write to us at the email below.

7. Age

The app is for people aged 18 and above. Younger users should use it only with a parent or guardian.

Never stop or change a medicine because of an app result. Talk to your doctor.
